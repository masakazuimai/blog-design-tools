What is clip-path?

clip-path clips the visible area of an element to a shape. You can use basic shapes like polygon(), circle(), ellipse() and inset() to mask images, cut diagonal sections, speech bubbles, arrows, stripes and checkerboards — with pure CSS and no image editing.

How to use (3 steps)

Tips & gotchas

  • Older browsers: include -webkit-clip-path (this tool adds it automatically).
  • Animation: to transition between two polygon shapes smoothly, keep the same number of points.
  • Shadows: clip-path also clips box-shadow. To keep a shadow, use filter: drop-shadow() on the parent.
