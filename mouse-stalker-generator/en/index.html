What is a mouse stalker?

A mouse stalker is an element that follows the cursor around the page: a circle, an image, particles or a trail. Effects that change shape over links also tell visitors what is clickable. Every effect here is plain code you can paste into any site: no build step, no jQuery, and it turns itself off on touch devices and for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
