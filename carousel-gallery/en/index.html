    Carousel types and when to use them

    A carousel shows images or cards in a row (or a column, or a spiral) that you swipe through or that move on their own. Use scroll snap for swiping, an infinite loop for things that move by themselves, and a spiral or coverflow where you want to catch the eye.

    Type Built with Browsers Good for
    Tornado, helix, double helix, swirlCSS only (@keyframes and transforms)All major browsersHero sections, portfolios
    Scroll-driven helix, scroll down to move sidewaysCSS (animation-timeline) + a little JSCSS in Chrome and Edge, JS elsewhereLong product stories
    Infinite, two-row, vertical, diagonal and wave loopsCSS onlyAll major browsersLogos, clients, testimonials, news
    Scroll snapCSS onlyAll major browsersMobile product and article lists

    Fan, accordionCSS only (:hover and transitions)All major browsersMenus and category entrances
